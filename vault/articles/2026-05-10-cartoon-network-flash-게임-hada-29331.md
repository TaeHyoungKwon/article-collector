## GeekNews 요약
- Web Design Museum의 **Cartoon Network Flash Games** 전시는 The Powerpuff Girls, Dexter’s Laboratory, Samurai Jack 등 Cartoon Network 시리즈에서 영감을 받은 브라우저 게임 모음을 소개함
- 전시는 **2001년** [Scooby-Doo: Scooby Snapshot](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/scooby-doo-scooby-snapshot-in-2001)부터 **2015년** [Sonic Boom: Link ‘N Smash’](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/sonic-boom-link-n-smash-in-2015), [The Amazing World of Gumball: Battle Bowlers](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/the-amazing-world-of-gumball-battle-bowlers-in-2015)까지 연도별로 구성됨
- **2002년**에는 [Batman: The Animated Series: The Riddler’s Secret Identity Inventor](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/batman-the-animated-series-the-riddlers-secret-identity-inventor-in-2002), [The Powerpuff Girls: Rescue from Townsville Zoo!](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/the-powerpuff-girls-rescue-from-townsville-zoo-in-2002), [Dexter’s Laboratory: Runaway Robot](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/dexters-laboratory-runaway-robot-in-2002) 등 여러 Cartoon Network Flash 게임이 포함됨
- **2003~2004년** 항목에는 [Samurai Jack: Code of the Samurai](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/samurai-jack-code-of-the-samurai-in-2003), [Scooby-Doo and the Creepy Castle](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/flash-game-exhibitions/scooby-doo-and-the-creepy-castle-in-2003), Foster’s Home for Imaginary Friends 기반 게임들이 이어짐
- **Ben 10**, **The Amazing World of Gumball**, Pokémon, Total Drama World Tour, Annoying Orange, Sonic Boom 관련 Flash 게임도 2006년 이후 목록에 포함됨
